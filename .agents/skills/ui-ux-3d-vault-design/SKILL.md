# UI/UX Pro Max: Classified Tactical Cyber-Noir Vault (گاوصندوق اسناد محرمانه)

## 1. Visual Identity: Tactical Cyber-Noir vs. Childish Cartoon
- **NO Cartoon / Pirate Gold**: Never use bright yellow, playful brass, or whimsical shapes.
- **Classified Detective / Military-Grade Ballistic Steel**:
  - Gunmetal, cold titanium, and tungsten steel (`#0a0f18`, `#161f2e`, `#2d3748`, `#475569`, `#94a3b8`, `#f1f5f9`).
  - Precision CNC chamfered seams, recessed hex bolts, and industrial hazard indices.
  - Tactical cybernetic status accents: Cold cyan (`#38bdf8`) / amber surveillance warning (`#f59e0b`) / laser reticle.
  - Hardened steel locking pistons with polished chrome specular highlights.
  - Heavy 6-spoke tactical steering rotary wheel with knurled diamond-texture steel handle grips and a deep armored cipher glass core containing the mysterious glowing `?` symbol.

## 2. Complete Mystery Hook (Zero Narrative Spoilers)
- The vault must be titled and presented as **«گاوصندوق اسناد محرمانه»** (Classified Documents Vault).
- **Never reveal or hint at the contents**: No mention of invitations, tickets, last session, or detectives. It stands as an impenetrable classified mystery from the first minute students see it.

## 3. High-Security Password Console Modal (`#vault-password-dialog`)
- Aesthetic: Classified Cryptographic Access Terminal (`ترمینال دسترسی اسناد محرمانه // SECURITY LEVEL 5`).
- Tactile mechanical/electronic keypad with crisp click audio and glowing LED feedback.
- On Wrong Code: Horizontal recoil vibration, crimson HUD pulse (`#ef4444`), low-frequency mechanical error buzz, and clear Persian feedback (`⛔ دسترسی غیرمجاز! رمز گاوصندوق اسناد محرمانه نادرست است.`).
- On Correct Code: Emerald status pulse (`✓ احراز هویت تایید شد`), immediate modal closure, returning full visual focus to the Slot 4 3D vault door.

## 4. 4-Stage Mechanical Choreography
1. Modal closes in 0.15s.
2. Slot 4 3D wheel turns slowly and deliberately over 3.5s (`rotate(540deg)`), accompanied by counter-rotating planetary gear teeth (`rotate(-360deg)`) and synchronized mechanical ratchet audio.
3. Heavy chromed steel locking pistons retract inward out of the frame.
4. Heavy 3D armored door swings open on its left hinges (`rotateY(-106deg)`), revealing the glowing classified document safe chamber inside.
5. Reward dossier modal (`#vault-reward-dialog`) opens presenting the unlocked document/poster with fullscreen view and download controls.
