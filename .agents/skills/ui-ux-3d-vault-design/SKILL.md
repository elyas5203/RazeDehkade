# UI/UX Pro Max: Real Physical Steel Vault (گاوصندوق واقعی اسناد محرمانه)

## 1. Visual Identity: Authentic Heavy Physical Safe vs. Childish Cartoons
- **NO Cartoon / Flash-Game / Arcade Neon**: Never use bright neon cyan glows, cartoon SVG cog gears, laser grids, or sci-fi video game graphics.
- **Real Industrial Commercial/Bank Steel Vault**:
  - Dark textured hammer-tone charcoal steel (`#12151a`, `#1b1f26`, `#262c36`, `#3b4351`).
  - Inset beveled heavy steel frame with realistic physical ambient shadows.
  - Two massive external solid steel barrel hinges on the left side with chrome pins and cast shadows.
  - Authentic mechanical combination dial with calibrated degree ticks (0 to 90) and diamond-knurled knob.
  - Solid chrome 3-spoke vault handle with real specular reflections and drop shadow on the door.
  - Cylindrical solid chrome locking bolts sliding in/out of heavy steel collars.

## 2. Complete Mystery Hook (Zero Narrative Spoilers)
- The vault is presented as **«گاوصندوق اسناد محرمانه»** (Classified Documents Vault).
- **Never reveal or hint at the contents**: No mention of invitations, tickets, last session, or detectives. It stands as an impenetrable classified mystery from the first minute students see it.

## 3. High-Security Password Console Modal (`#vault-password-dialog`)
- Aesthetic: Classified Security Terminal (`ترمینال امنیتی اسناد محرمانه // SECURITY LEVEL 5`).
- Tactile metal keypad with realistic styling.
- On Wrong Code: Horizontal recoil vibration and clear Persian feedback (`⛔ دسترسی غیرمجاز! رمز گاوصندوق اسناد محرمانه نادرست است.`).
- On Correct Code: Immediate modal closure, returning full visual focus to the Slot 4 physical vault door.

## 4. Zero Arcade / Game Audio Policy
- NO arcade synth sounds, NO "دیدیدینگ" victory chords, NO video game bleeps. The physical mechanism operates realistically and silently.

## 5. Physical Mechanical Unlocking Sequence
1. Modal closes instantly.
2. Slot 4 solid chrome 3-spoke handle rotates smoothly under physical weight.
3. Heavy cylindrical chrome deadbolts retract into the door.
4. Heavy 3D armored door swings open on its left barrel hinges (`rotateY(-105deg)`), revealing the interior classified safe shelf.
5. Reward dossier modal (`#vault-reward-dialog`) opens presenting the unlocked document/poster with fullscreen view and download controls.
